# Math Batch 1 Audit (math_batch1.json, 30 items)

Auditor scope: I recomputed every key and checked every distractor note against the arithmetic it claims. I also checked objective fit, Supply-answer accepted forms, clueing and level. The file was not edited.

## Bottom line

- **Keys:** All 30 keys are correct, and each item has only one defensible answer. Recomputed values: 0.375; 4.5%; 11/12; 6; order C,A,B,D; 1/9; x=6; x=5; 770 mL; $1,170; $45; 10%; 3.48; 300; 5 cups; 8 mL; 2:3; 60 mph; 50+25m; 150+15h≥600; 1.8 °F; Mar→Apr; 8; A,C; positive; 8; 180 ft²; 31.4 cm; 75 kg; 240 mL.
- **Must-fix:** none.
- **Should-fix:** 10 items. The main problem is distractor notes that describe made-up error paths. A note sometimes names an error that does not produce the value, or that no student would make. All items are tagged diagnostic, so these notes would give students wrong feedback. The other problems are twin options that a student can eliminate as a pair, and objective-fit gaps in M.1.7 and M.2.1.
- **Key balance (MC):** A=6, B=6, C=6, D=6. No position cue.

## Global notes

1. **Accepted forms (Supply answer):** The answer lists are enumerated and inconsistent. 8 mL accepts "8mL" but not "8 ml"; 240 accepts "240 ml" but not "240ml"; nothing accepts trailing zeros (4.50, 5.0). Fix this in the grader rather than in each item. Strip whitespace, case, a trailing unit or %, and a leading "x="; then compare numerically. All four keyed values are correct, and none of the forms they accept is wrong. (optional, but do it once at the grader level)
2. **Level and calculator:** TEAS 7 allows an on-screen calculator. Most items here are single-step at difficulty 1–2: M.1.5-001, M.1.2-002, M.2.2-001, M.2.4-001 and M.2.5-002 all become trivial button presses. The real exam leans toward multi-step applied problems, such as dosage plus conversion, percent change with a table, and multi-step expressions. For diagnostic use this batch is a reasonable floor. The bank needs more difficulty-3, multi-step items in later batches, especially for M.1.5, M.1.6, M.1.8 and M.2.5. (should-fix at the bank level, not per item)
3. **Distractor-note standard:** Every note that says "an addition/subtraction/division error" should name the operation that yields the value. Otherwise the option should be replaced with one whose path can be named. All items are tagged diagnostic, so the notes are likely to drive the feedback students see.
