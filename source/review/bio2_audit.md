# Biology Batch 2 Audit (sci_bio_batch2.json, 24 items)

Auditor scope: I checked every key against intro-level biology (OpenStax *Concepts of Biology*). I recomputed every genetics probability and checked each explanation and distractor note, including that the option letters match. I also checked objective fit and clueing. The file was not edited.

## Bottom line

- **Keys:** All 24 keys are correct, and each is the only defensible option given. Recomputed genetics:
  - S.2.3-004: Cc × Cc gives P(Cc) = 2/4 = 1/2.
  - S.2.3-005: XᴴXʰ × XᴴY gives P(affected | son) = 1/2. P(affected son) among all children = 1/4, which matches the note for B.
  - S.2.3-006: AO × BO gives P(OO) = 1/4.
  - S.2.3-007: RW × RW gives P(RW) = 1/2. 1/4 is red or white; 3/4 is the 3:1 pattern.
  - S.2.3-008: Tt × tt gives 1/2 short, so the tall parent is Tt.
  - The ordered-response key for S.2.1-009 (B, D, A, C) resolves to Prophase, Metaphase, Anaphase, Telophase, which is correct.
- **All distractor-note letters match their options.**
- **Must-fix:** 1 item (S.2.5-004). The key letter is fine, but the explanation states a contested claim as fact.
- **Should-fix:** 3 items (S.2.4-005, S.2.4-006, S.2.2-007).
- **Key balance (21 single-answer MC):** A=7, B=4, C=5, D=5. A is slightly heavy, partly because S.2.2-007 puts "3" first (see below). Both select-all items are keyed **A, C, E**.
