## Must-fix

| Page | Quoted text | Problem | Suggested fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| S.4.1 | Prefix table lists `micro-` directly under `milli-`; hook ends "...= kilo, hecto, deka, Base, deci, centi, milli. Each step is a factor of 10." | Table puts micro one row below milli, and "each step is ×10" invites mg→mcg = ×10. That is a medication-math error (it is ×1,000). Also the table omits hecto/deka, so "2.5 kg → g: 3 steps" doesn't match the table (kilo is one row above base). | Add hecto (100×) and deka (10×) rows to the table. After the hook add: "Micro is **3 steps below milli**: 1 mg = 1,000 mcg." Consider one worked mg→mcg example. |
| S.3.4 | Table: "Temperature · Higher · **Faster**"; then "Enzymes are the body's catalysts; they let reactions run fast at 37 °C." | No caveat; contradicts S.2.4 (denaturation above optimum) and S.4.3 ("Enzyme activity vs temperature: hill-shaped"). Student may infer a fever speeds all enzymes. | Add to the enzyme bullet: "but too much heat (or the wrong pH) **denatures** an enzyme and the reaction slows (see S.2.4)." Optionally add as a Common trap. |
