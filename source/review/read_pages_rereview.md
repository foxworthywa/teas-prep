## 3. SHOULD items still unaddressed (student and content reports)

None of these is a correctness error. Three are worth a one-line fix because a weak reader could misread a Check:

- **R.3.3 Check 1:** "students who used it three times were more likely to pass" can be read as "three times more likely." Change it to "**at least three times**." The key's "pass-rate result" also refers to a rate the question never gives. Use "the finding that users were more likely to pass."
- **R.3.4 Check 1:** "fast" isn't defined on this page, and EAL readers may read it as "quick." Add "(not eat)." R.2.2 defines *fasting*, but students may not have read that page.
- **R.1.1 Check 2 key:** "Choice (a) is too vague" uses a category the §3 table doesn't teach. Better: "Choice (a) leaves out the training and the win, the key change and the outcome."

These are lower value and fine to leave:
- **R.1.2 and R.3.1:** "Using outside knowledge ✗" sits awkwardly with the Maya foot-tapping inference, which uses everyday knowledge.
- **R.3.1:** "someone who fixed a failure once…" still refers to an unseen passage, and the "two to three years" trap is still cryptic. The page was not revised.
- **R.1.3:** "prohibition" and "order" are hard words for an EAL reader.
- **R.1.5:** "axes" isn't defined, and ≥/> aren't put into words.
- **R.2.3:** "counterargument" is used before it's defined.
- **R.2.4 §3:** the bus-company example still assumes an unseen passage.
- **R.3.3:** counterargument and rebuttal share one definition.
- **X4:** the free-response keys don't list other accepted wordings. R.1.1 Check 1 and R.3.2 Check 1 do include alternates now.

These are coverage questions, not page errors. Confirm them against the ATI TEAS 7 blueprint before adding anything:

- figurative language (R.2.2);

## 4. New problems introduced by the revisions

1. **R.2.1: the Checks are out of order on the page.** The new stereotype Check in §3 is labeled **Check 3**, but it comes before **Check 2** (misconceptions, §4). The answer list runs 1, 2, 3, so answer 2 matches a Check that comes later on the page. An anxious reader will think something is missing. **Fix:** renumber the §3 stereotype Check as Check 2 and the §4 misconception Check as Check 3, then swap answers 2 and 3:
   - "2. **(b).** It judges all older people by one trait; (a) says only 'some.'"
   - "3. **That 'natural' means 'safe.'** Natural products can still cause side effects."
2. **R.3.2 §1: unclear "It."** The new nonfiction sentence was inserted between the definition and "It's a **complete thought**, not a single word." Now "It" seems to point to "main ideas and values." **Fix:** move the sentence up. "A **theme** is the message or lesson about life that a story suggests. It's a **complete thought**, not a single word. With nonfiction, such as opinion pieces, compare **main ideas** and **values** (beliefs about what is important) instead."
