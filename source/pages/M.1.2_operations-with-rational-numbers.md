# Arithmetic with Fractions, Decimals and Negative Numbers

**TEAS objective M.1.2** · Number and Algebra

**What the TEAS expects:** you can add, subtract, multiply and divide fractions, decimals and signed numbers, and use the order of operations.


**Calculator:** the TEAS gives you a basic on-screen calculator. It may not follow the order of operations and shows fractions as decimals, so do PEMDAS one step at a time and convert back to a fraction when the answer choices are fractions.

---

## 1. Order of operations: PEMDAS

1. **P**arentheses (and other grouping symbols)
2. **E**xponents
3. **M**ultiplication and **D**ivision, **left to right**
4. **A**ddition and **S**ubtraction, **left to right**

(An **exponent** means repeated multiplication: 4² = 4 × 4 = 16.)

**Memory hook:** Please Excuse My Dear Aunt Sally. Multiplication does **not** always come before division; whichever comes first, left to right, goes first.

**Example:** 20 − 3 × (4 − 2)² = 20 − 3 × 2² = 20 − 3 × 4 = 20 − 12 = **8**.

## 2. Fractions

| Operation | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Add or subtract | Get a **common denominator** (rewrite both fractions with the same bottom number), then add or subtract the tops | 1/2 + 1/3 = 3/6 + 2/6 = **5/6** |
| Multiply | Top × top, bottom × bottom | 2/3 × 3/4 = 6/12 = **1/2** |
| Divide | **Keep, change, flip**: multiply by the **reciprocal** (the second fraction flipped upside down) | 3/4 ÷ 1/2 = 3/4 × 2/1 = 6/4 = **1 1/2** |

With mixed numbers, convert to improper fractions before multiplying or dividing: 1 1/2 × 2 2/3 = 3/2 × 8/3 = 24/6 = **4**.

## 3. Decimals

- **Add or subtract:** line up the decimal points. 12.5 + 3.75 = **16.25**.
- **Multiply:** multiply as whole numbers, then count the total decimal places in both factors. 0.3 × 0.02: 3 × 2 = 6, with 3 decimal places → **0.006**.
- **Divide:** move the decimal point in the divisor to make it whole, and move the dividend's point the same number of places. 4.5 ÷ 0.15 = 450 ÷ 15 = **30**.

---

## 4. Negative numbers

**Adding:** same signs → add and keep the sign (−4 + −6 = **−10**). Different signs → subtract the smaller **absolute value** (distance from zero, ignoring the sign) from the larger, and keep the sign of the number that is farther from zero (−9 + 4 = **−5**).

**Subtracting:** add the opposite. 3 − (−5) = 3 + 5 = **8**.

**Multiplying and dividing:**
- Same signs → **positive** (−6 × −3 = 18)
- Different signs → **negative** (−20 ÷ 4 = −5)

## Common traps

- **Adding denominators.** 1/2 + 1/3 is not 2/5.
- **Flipping the wrong fraction when dividing.** Flip the second one (the divisor).
- **Doing multiplication before an earlier division.** Go left to right.
- **Losing track of signs.** Two negatives multiply to a positive; subtracting a negative means adding.
