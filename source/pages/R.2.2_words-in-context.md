# Word Meaning from Context

**TEAS objective R.2.2** · Craft and Structure

**What the TEAS expects:** you can figure out what a word or phrase means from the sentences around it.

---

## 1. Kinds of context clues

| Clue | Example | Meaning of the bold word |
|---|---|---|
| **Definition** | ***Fasting***, or going without food, is required before the test. | going without food |
| **Example** | ***Citrus*** fruits, such as oranges, lemons, and limes, are high in vitamin C. | a group of sour, juicy fruits |
| **Contrast** | Online tutoring is not a ***luxury***; it is a necessity. | something nice but not necessary |
| **Restatement or explanation** | The wound was ***superficial***; it barely broke the skin. | on the surface, not deep |
| **Cause and effect** | Because the medicine works only ***temporarily***, the dose is repeated daily. | for a short time |

---

## 2. The substitution test

1. Cover the word.
2. Think of your own word that fits the sentence.
3. Plug each answer choice into the sentence. Keep the one that **fits the meaning**, not just the grammar.

> **Check 1:** *The new aide was meticulous; she checked every chart twice and never missed a detail.* What does **meticulous** most nearly mean?

## 3. Words with more than one meaning

Many test words are common words used in a **less common sense**. "Humble" can describe a modest person, but in *the humble potato feeds much of the world* it means **ordinary**. Always choose the meaning that fits **this** sentence.

> **Check 2:** *The report was dry, full of tables and no stories.* Does **dry** mean "not wet" here?
> *Answer at the bottom of the page.*

---

## 4. Word parts help too

Prefixes, roots, and suffixes (see the E.3.2 study page) can confirm a guess: *temporarily* → *tempor* (time) + *-ary* (relating to) + *-ly* (in a way) → for a time.

---

## 5. Connotation

Words with similar meanings can carry different feelings. *Thrifty* and *cheap* both mean spending little, but *thrifty* sounds positive and *cheap* sounds negative. When a question asks what a word suggests, think about the feeling as well as the meaning.

---

## Common traps

- **Choosing the most common meaning** instead of the meaning in context.
- **Choosing a choice related to the topic** but not to the word.
- **Not rereading the sentence** with your answer in place.
