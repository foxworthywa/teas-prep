# Converting Fractions, Decimals and Percents

**TEAS objective M.1.1** · Number and Algebra

**What the TEAS expects:** you can move quickly between fractions, decimals and percents, including mixed numbers.


**Calculator:** the TEAS gives you a basic on-screen calculator. Use it to divide a fraction's top by its bottom.

---

## 1. The conversions you need

| From → To | How | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Fraction → decimal | **Divide** the top by the bottom | 3/8 = 3 ÷ 8 = **0.375** |
| Decimal → percent | **Multiply by 100** (move the decimal point **2 places right**) and add % | 0.375 → **37.5%** |
| Percent → decimal | **Divide by 100** (move the decimal point **2 places left**) | 6% → **0.06** |
| Decimal → fraction | Write over 10, 100, 1,000… by place value, then simplify | 0.45 = 45/100 = **9/20** |
| Percent → fraction | Write over 100, then simplify | 35% = 35/100 = **7/20** |
| Fraction → percent | Divide, then multiply by 100 | 3/5 = 0.6 = **60%** |

**Memory hook:** "percent" means "per hundred." 45% = 45 per 100 = 45/100.

**Careful with small percents:** 0.5% = 0.005, not 0.5. A 7.5% sales tax is 0.075.

> **Check 1:** Write 0.08 as a percent and as a fraction in simplest form.

## 2. Mixed numbers and improper fractions

- **Mixed → improper:** multiply the whole number by the denominator, add the numerator, keep the denominator. 2 3/4 = (2 × 4 + 3)/4 = **11/4**.
- **Improper → mixed:** divide. 17/5 = 3 remainder 2 = **3 2/5**.
- **Mixed → decimal:** convert just the fraction part. 2 3/4 = 2 + 0.75 = **2.75**.

---

## 3. Simplifying fractions

Divide the top and bottom by their **greatest common factor** (the largest number that divides evenly into both). 18/24: both divide by 6 → **3/4**.

## Common traps

- **Moving the decimal point the wrong way.** Decimal → percent makes the number **bigger** (0.25 → 25%).
- **Forgetting the zero in small percents.** 4% = 0.04, not 0.4.
- **Dividing the wrong way.** 3/8 is 3 ÷ 8, not 8 ÷ 3.
- **Not simplifying.** The TEAS answer choices are usually in simplest form.
