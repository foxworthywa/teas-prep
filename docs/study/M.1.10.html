Expressions, Equations and Inequalities in Real Life

TEAS objective M.1.10 · Number and Algebra

What the TEAS expects: you can translate a real situation into an expression, equation or inequality, evaluate it, and solve simple inequalities.

Calculator: the TEAS gives you a basic on-screen calculator. Use it after you have set up the equation.

1. Translating words into algebra

Words Algebra
5 more than a number x + 5
5 less than a number x − 5 (order flips!)
twice a number 2x
a number divided by 4 x/4
3 times the sum of a number and 2 3(x + 2)
is, equals, will be =
at least, no less than, minimum ≥
at most, no more than, maximum ≤
is more than, is greater than, exceeds >
is less than, is fewer than <

Watch for "is." "5 less than x" is an expression (x − 5). "5 is less than x" is an inequality (5 < x).

Fixed + variable cost model: total = (one-time amount) + (rate × number of units).

  • A gym charges $50 to join plus $25 a month: C = 50 + 25m.
  • A taxi charges $3 plus $2.50 per mile: C = 3 + 2.5d.

Check 1: A phone plan costs $30 a month plus $0.10 per text. Write an expression for one month's cost with t texts, then evaluate it for 150 texts. Answer at the bottom of the page.

2. Evaluating expressions

Substitute, then follow the order of operations.

  • For 3x² − 2x with x = 4: 3(16) − 8 = 48 − 8 = 40.
  • Use parentheses when substituting negatives: x = −2 in x² gives (−2)² = 4.

3. Solving inequalities

Story: Maria needs at least $600 for tuition. She has $150 saved and earns $15 an hour. How many hours, h, must she work?

Solve like an equation, except: when you multiply or divide by a negative number, flip the inequality sign.

  • 150 + 15h ≥ 600 → 15h ≥ 450 → h ≥ 30
  • −3x < 12 → divide by −3 and flip → x > −4

Reading the answer in context: h ≥ 30 means she must work at least 30 hours.

Check 2: Solve: 7 − 2x ≤ 1. Answer at the bottom of the page.

4. Writing an equation from a story

Example: a rental costs $40 plus $0.25 per mile. The bill was $65. How many miles?

  • 40 + 0.25m = 65 → 0.25m = 25 → m = 100 miles

Common traps

  • Reversing "less than." "5 less than x" is x − 5, not 5 − x.
  • Choosing ≤ for "at least." At least means ≥.
  • Forgetting to flip the sign when dividing by a negative.
  • Charging a one-time fee every month. The fixed part isn't multiplied by the variable.
